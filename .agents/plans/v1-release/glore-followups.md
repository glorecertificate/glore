# GloRe team follow-ups

Items the GloRe team owns before or soon after the v1 launch. Engineering cannot settle them.

## Before launch

### 1. Legal texts

- Review the privacy policy, terms of use, and Quality Charter in en, es, and it (`content/legal/*.md`). They are drafts written for v1.
- Confirm the contact person, address, email provider, legal bases, and retention periods.
- Confirm international transfers, the data protection officer, liability, and governing law.
- Confirm the time a review may take and the consequences of a charter breach.

### 2. Skill framework

Confirm the group and definition of four skills without a published GloRe definition. All four sit in "Work skills".

- Problem solving: "Understand a challenge before you act, come up with several options, and pick a solution that fits your time, people and resources."
- Decision making: "Compare your options, choose in time with the information you have, and stand behind the result, even when you cannot be sure."
- Initiative: "Notice what needs doing, start it without waiting to be asked, and know when to check in with others first."
- Work ethic and commitment: "Put in steady, honest effort, do the work well when nobody is checking, and keep your promises or say early when you cannot."

### 3. Skill names and email sender

- Check the es and it names of the 25 skills (`content/courses/*/course.json`, `content/skill-groups.json`). They follow the glorecertificate.net skill pages where those exist.
- Confirm the sender name, the sender mailbox, and the support address users reply to.

## At launch

### 4. Course publishing

Production imports every course as a draft. GloRe editors review and publish each one per language.

- Skill courses: all 25.
- Intro courses: Welcome to GloRe, Soft skills and volunteering.
- Learner courses: Volunteering abroad with ESC and Erasmus+, Intercultural learning, Volunteer wellbeing and safety, Planning a community project.

### 5. Help center and partners

- Review the 33 docs (4 intro, 7 tutorials, 22 FAQ) in the three languages, checking facts such as cost and the activity rule.
- Ask partner organizations to add their logo and signatory in Organization settings before approving certificates. Both appear on the PDF.

## After launch

### 6. Translations

- Name the GloRe owner of the Crowdin project (`crowdin.yml`), who invites translators and approves translation pull requests.
- Courses and docs are translated in the app editors, not in Crowdin.
