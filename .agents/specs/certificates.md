# Certificates

Certificate lifecycle, screens, PDF, and public verification. Roles and the other domain rules are in `app.md`.

## Lifecycle

**Eligibility:** a volunteer can request a certificate after completing at least `minSkills` (3) skill courses with an average assessment rating of at least `minRating` (3 of 5) across completed skill courses. Assessment ratings of 1 to 5 come from the final assessment of each skill course.

**Status flow:**

```
draft -> submitted -> in_review -> approved -> revoked
         submitted | in_review -> changes_requested -> (resubmit) -> submitted
         open status -> withdrawn (volunteer) | rejected (assigned tutor, terminal)
```

Open statuses are `submitted`, `in_review`, and `changes_requested`. A user has at most one open request per organization (D4); approved certificates stack for new periods. Every transition runs through `src/actions/certificates/{requests,assignment,review}.ts`, records an `activity_events` row in the same transaction, and calls `notify`.

**Request:** only a `volunteer` of the active org creates drafts (up to 5) and submits. Submit validates 1 to 4 tasks of at most 110 characters, 3 to 4 completed skill courses (checked fresh), the experience rating, eligibility (D8), the activity rule (100 hours, or an end date three months after the start; error `ACTIVITY_TOO_SHORT`), and the personal data (last name, birthday, birth place, nationality; the volunteer may fill them inline, error `PERSONAL_DATA_MISSING`). A missing `selfLevel` comes from the course assessment average. Submit generates the public `code` and keeps it on resubmit.

**Reviewer assignment:** submit and resubmit auto-assign the org tutor with the fewest open requests, ties to the oldest last assignment (never assigned counts as oldest); authors and banned users are skipped, and an org without tutors leaves the request in the pool. Resubmit keeps the reviewer while still assignable. Org admins, representatives, and platform admins assign, reassign, and unassign; the assigned tutor can release. An `in_review` request goes back to `submitted` when its tutor changes, while `changes_requested` keeps its status. Tutors self-assign unassigned `submitted` requests of their org. The tutor queue is scoped to the active org, and drafts are visible to their owner only. ONLY tutors review; admins and representatives manage assignment but never review directly.

**Review:** the assigned tutor claims the request (`in_review`), requests changes (section comments stored on the review row), rejects (reason of 10 or more characters, from any open status), or approves. Approval merges the tutor's edits, requires levels 2 to 5 on 3 to 4 skills, rechecks the activity rule, renders the PDF, commits the status, review row, and event in one transaction, then uploads to R2; an upload failure reverts the whole approval and returns `STORAGE_FAILED`. The first approved certificate becomes the default on the public profile. Org managers and platform admins revoke an approved certificate with a reason of 10 or more characters; the PDF stays in R2 and the latest approved certificate becomes the default.

## Screens

- **Request wizard** (`/certificates/new`, volunteers of the active org): Experience (dates, hours, location, 1 to 4 tasks of 110 characters), Skills (3 to 4 completed skill courses; self-level prefilled from the course assessment and adjustable, reflection prefilled as evidence), Your organization (anonymous rating, shown to the org only as an aggregate of 3 or more), Review (language from the UI locale, missing personal data inline, live `CertificateThumbnail`). Drafts autosave through `saveCertificateDraft`; `?draft=<id>` resumes a draft, and a `changes_requested` id opens the same wizard in resubmit mode with the tutor's comments. `EligibilityPanel` replaces the wizard when eligibility fails or a request is already open.
- **Wallet** (`/certificates`, volunteers): `CertificateCard` shows a `CertificatePreview` miniature with status, period, and hours; the featured (`isDefault`) approved certificate leads as a `FeaturedCredentialCard`; `EligibilityPanel` replaces the page when nothing exists and the volunteer is not eligible. `featureCertificate` moves the flag between approved certificates.
- **Detail** (`/certificates/[id]`): status header with a one-line message, `CertificateJourney` (draft, submitted, review, issued; the stage where a request ended shows its outcome), reviewer feedback (`CertificateFeedback`, general and per-section notes, rejection and revocation reasons), the reviewer row, the PDF miniature with the summary, and a timeline from `findCertificateActivity`. Actions follow status and role: continue or resubmit through the wizard, withdraw (open), download and make featured (approved), revoke (managers), open review (assigned tutor). The owner of an approved certificate also gets `CertificateShare` (verify link, QR, PDF, LinkedIn "Add to profile" link with `organizationName` and copy fields, LinkedIn and Facebook share) and a one-time confetti per certificate (`localStorage` flag).
- **Tutor queue** (`/certificates`, tutors): tabs assigned to me (open), unassigned (org pool, self-claim), and completed, in a `DataTable` with search and status and waiting-time filters as nuqs params (`tab`, `q`, `status`, `age`). The age badge counts days since `submittedAt` and turns due at 3 days and overdue at 7 (`reviewReminderDays`); `changes_requested` rows show "waiting for volunteer".
- **Review workspace** (`/certificates/[id]/review`, the assigned tutor only): left, the volunteer, experience, tasks, and skills with self level, evidence, and course results (`findReviewContext`) plus the previous feedback; right, the form (editable experience and tasks, per-skill include, confirmed level of at least Intermediate and a 200 character statement, four blind scores, a note per section, a general comment). Approve first renders the real PDF with `previewCertificatePdf` (persists nothing) in a dialog, then calls `approveCertificate`. Request changes needs a general comment or a section note. Reject needs a reason of at least 10 characters. `Start review` claims a `submitted` request.
- **Organization pipeline** (`OrganizationCertificates` in `features/certificates`): four `StatCard`s (submitted, in review, changes requested, approved), search, status and reviewer filters held in local state (no URL params, so it can sit in a tab), and a `DataTable` with reassign, revoke, and view per row. Props: `certificates` from `listOrganizationCertificates(orgId)` (non-draft, org admins, representatives, and platform admins only) and `tutors` from `listOrgTutors(orgId)`. `/certificates` mounts it for the active org's admins and representatives; the organization tab and `/admin/certificates?org=ID` embed it through `OrganizationCertificatesPanel`. Platform admins reach `/certificates/[id]` (the `canEdit` guard in the route layout lets admins through) and `/certificates` redirects them to `/admin/certificates`.
- Form errors are `Certificates.errors.*` codes set as zod messages and rendered by `FieldError`. `new/values.ts` maps form values to `CertificateEditValues` and back, and drops blank tasks.

**PDF:** generated on approval and stored at `certificates/{handle}.pdf` in R2, public via `documentUrl`. It follows the A4 template (Roboto, navy and green tokens, vector logo, `DD.MM.YYYY` dates) and holds a QR code and short URL pointing to `/verify/<code>`, plus the signatory block.

**Public verification:** `/verify` takes a code (any case, dashes optional, normalized by `normalizeCertificateCode`) and redirects to `/verify/<code>`, which shows the holder's full name, organization and logo, period, hours, location, tasks, skills with levels and statements, issue date, signatory, PDF link, QR, an Open Graph image, and JSON-LD. Both routes are public, `noindex`, and rate limited per IP (60 checks and 30 lookups a minute). An approved certificate reads `valid`; a revoked one reads `revoked` with its date and shows only the holder, organization, code, and issue date (never its reason, PDF link, tasks, skills, signatory, or QR; `/api/v1/certificates/<id>/pdf` answers 410); every other status is `unknown`. The page reads the issued `snapshot`, so later profile edits never change it. `/{username}` is opt-in (`users.publicProfile`, default off) and shows the featured certificate; a private profile is a 404, and the legacy `/{username}?v={handle}` permanently redirects to `/verify/<code>`.

**Account deletion:** `eraseAccount` (`deleteUser` in `db/mutations/user.ts`) deletes the holder's drafts and open requests, and anonymizes every issued (`approved` or `revoked`) certificate in the same transaction instead of deleting it. The row keeps the organization, `code`, `handle`, dates, hours, `reviewerId`, status, skills with `level` and `selfLevel`, review scores, and rating scores, so organization stats and tutor history still count it. `user_id` becomes null, `holder_deleted_at` is set (a dedicated marker, so `revoked` keeps meaning an organization decision), `is_default` is cleared, and the activity description, location, tasks, `snapshot` (holder name, birth data, tasks, statements), reviewer, rejection, and revocation texts, skill `evidence` and `statement`, review comments, the rating comment, and the free-text keys of its events (`comment`, `reason`, `holderId`, `documentUrl`) are scrubbed. After the commit the PDF is removed from R2 (`releaseUserFiles`, which also expires the organization and tutor caches); a failed delete is left to the daily prune. `/verify/<code>` then answers `holder_deleted` (`verifyCertificate`; `findCertificateByCode` returns `{ code, status: 'holder_deleted' }`) and renders only a "no longer valid" notice and the code, with a generic Open Graph image and no JSON-LD; the legacy `?v=` link still redirects there. `OrganizationCertificates` shows "Deleted user" and drops the view and revoke actions. The anonymized row has no detail page, no PDF route, no search hit, and never reaches the tutor queue; lifecycle actions refuse it (`findCertificateRow` and `transition` need a holder). Code that reads `certificates.userId` must treat null as a deleted holder.
