# Organizations

Organization domain rules: invitations, members, ownership, settings, insights, and applications. Product context: `app.md`.

## Invitations and invite links

- Invitations never create memberships or accounts for an existing person; accepting one does (`src/db/mutations/invitation.ts`). Inviting records a token, role, optional message, and an expiry of `invitationExpiryDays`, and mails `/invite/[token]?proof=...`. The row carries `userId` when an account with that email exists, still without a membership.
- `/invite/[token]` is public and noindex. A new person creates the account only with the emailed `proof`, a second one-time secret kept hashed in `verifications` (`org-invite:<id>`); a link without it offers to mail a fresh proof link (`requestInvitationProof`, rate limited), so a copied link alone never yields a verified account. The new account lands on onboarding; an existing account signs in, then accepts or declines; another signed-in account sees a masked-email mismatch with sign out. Expired, revoked, accepted, and declined tokens show a closed state. Declining works signed out.
- Managers (`admin`, `representative`) invite one person or paste up to `maxBulkInvites` (per-row results), then resend (60s cooldown, rotates the token and proof), revoke, or copy the link from the Invitations tab. The panel payload never holds a token: copying calls `copyOrganizationInvitationLink` or `copyOrganizationInviteLink`, which re-check the role (`canManageInvitation`) and return the link without the proof. A representative cannot invite `admin` or `representative`, and a second `admin` is refused (`ROLE_TAKEN`).
- Invite links (`/join/[token]`, learner or volunteer only) carry `maxUses`, an expiry, and optional approval. A signed-in person joins, or files a request tied to their `userId`. A guest gives name and email and receives an invitation by mail, which verifies the address; replies are generic so a link never reveals whether an address is a member.
- Approving a join request that has a `userId` (filed signed in) adds the membership. Approving an anonymous one sends an invitation instead, so only the person's own acceptance links an existing account.
- Revoking an invitation or link ends its token at once. Removing a member (`removeMembership`) also revokes the links and open invitations that person created, and deletes their unaccepted legacy join tokens when no membership or team role remains.
- Organization approval, platform-admin organization invites, and admin member invites send an `admin` or member invitation. None creates an account or a membership. Public actions are rate limited (`invite-account`, `invite-proof`, `invite-decline`, `join-link`; invite sign-in shares the `login` keys).

## Members and ownership

- `/organization?tab=members` is a `DataTable` named "Members" with search, role and status filters, sorting, and pagination. "Details" opens a drawer with the profile, learning progress, and the member's non-draft certificates in this org (`getOrganizationMemberDetail`, managers only).
- Only the owner changes roles, and never to or from `admin`; the owner role moves through an ownership transfer. Managers remove members (a representative only learners, tutors, and volunteers). Nobody removes themselves; a member leaves through `leaveOrganization`, which also moves the active org cookie.
- Transfer (`transferOrganizationOwnership`): the owner picks an onboarded `representative` and types the organization name. One transaction swaps the roles and records `organization.ownership_transferred`, which mails both people. The last owner can neither leave nor be removed (`LAST_OWNER`).
- Demoting, removing, or losing a tutor returns their open reviews (`submitted`, `in_review`, `changes_requested`) to the pool through `certificate.unassigned`; approved certificates keep their reviewer. Role changes record `member.role_changed` and leaving records `member.left`.
- Every org action takes an optional `organizationId`, resolved by `getOrganizationAccess`: members act on orgs they belong to, and platform admins act as owner on any org (never on ownership transfers).

## Settings and signatory

- `OrganizationSettings` serves `/organization?tab=settings` and `/admin/organizations/[id]?tab=settings`; only the owner (or a platform admin through `organizationId`) edits. Sections: branding, details, per-locale description, legal identity (`legalName`, `registryId`), certificate signatory, contact, danger zone.
- The description form sends only the locales the user edited; `mergeDescription` merges them into the stored record under a row lock, so an edit in one language never overwrites another, and a blank value removes that locale.
- The signatory is `organization_profiles.legalRepresentative` (printed after "Represented by") plus an optional PNG or JPG `signatureUrl` (R2 prefix `signatures/<orgId>-`, presigned upload, `r2PresignSignature`). The PDF also reads `avatarUrl` as the logo; `pdfImage` skips `.webp`. A certificate with a stored `snapshot` regenerates with the snapshot's signatory, so changes reach only new approvals.
- Deleting an organization is refused with `HAS_CERTIFICATES` once any certificate exists; otherwise it removes the row, expires member access caches, and deletes the logo.

## Overview, insights, and activity

- The overview opens with a first-run checklist (`buildChecklist` in `src/db/queries/organization-insights.ts`): profile and signatory (legal name, registry ID, representative), a tutor (member or pending invitation), volunteers (same), and a first non-draft certificate. It disappears once all four hold. The panel payload (`getOrganizationPanel`) also carries the certificate `pipeline` by status and the first activity page, never tokens.
- `?tab=certificates` renders `OrganizationCertificatesSlot` (the pipeline counts) until the certificate lane mounts `OrganizationCertificates` there.
- `?tab=insights` loads `getOrganizationInsights(organizationId?)` on demand (managers only): learning progress of learners and volunteers, certificates submitted and approved per month (`Chart`, 12 months), average review time, tutor workload (open and reviewed), and an anonymous rating aggregate from `organization_ratings` that stays hidden below `RATING_MIN_COUNT` (3) ratings. Comments are never returned.
- `?tab=activity` pages `activity_events` by organization (`getOrganizationActivity(cursor?, organizationId?)`, 20 per page, newest first). `listOrganizationActivity` resolves actor, target, and tutor names on the server; the client renders `activity_<action with dots as underscores>` messages and falls back to `activity_unknown`, so a new event type only needs a message. The admin organization page reuses the same action with `organizationId`.

## Applications

- `/register` is a four-step application (`features/organization/application/`): organization (name, legal name, registry ID, country, city, email, website, volunteers per year), legal representative plus an optional contact person, motivation, then a review with the required Quality Charter checkbox (link to `/quality-charter` in a new tab). The zod schema in `src/db/queries/organization-application.ts` is shared by the form and the server; its messages are error codes that the form translates.
- `requestOrganizationRegistration` rate limits by IP and email, then `submitOrganizationApplication` creates the unapproved organization, fills the profile (`legalName`, `registryId`, `legalRepresentative`, contact, `volunteersPerYear`, `motivation`, `charterAcceptedAt`), files the representative's pending `admin` join request, and records `organization.application_received`, which mails the applicant a receipt and the platform admins.
- Platform admins review in `/admin/organizations`: the row menu opens an application sheet (all submitted data). Approving sends the representative an invitation to `/invite/[token]` (no account is created or linked before they accept). Rejecting is a mutation (`rejectOrganizationApplication`) that keeps the organization, marks the request rejected, and records `organization.application_decided` with `approved: false`.
- `/admin/organizations/[id]` reuses the organization components with `organizationId`: the same members, invitations (without invite links), settings, and activity tabs, plus an application tab for organizations that applied.
