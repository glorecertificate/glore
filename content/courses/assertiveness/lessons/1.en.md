---
title: The fourth extra shift
---

Tomás is a volunteer at a community sports club in Porto. He coaches the under-twelves on Saturdays and he loves it. The club is short of helpers this month, so the coordinator, Inês, keeps asking him to cover more: a Sunday tournament, a Wednesday training, and now a fourth extra shift.

Tomás has an exam on Monday. He feels his stomach tighten when Inês asks, and he hears himself say "Sure, no problem." Then he spends the evening annoyed, at her and at himself.

The next week she asks again. This time Tomás takes a breath.

"Inês, I want to help the club, and I know you are under pressure. I can't take the Sunday shift because I have an exam on Monday. I can do the Wednesday training. Could we look together for someone else for Sunday?"

Inês looks surprised, then relieved. She says she had not realised how much he had already taken on. They ask the parents' group, and a father offers to help on Sunday.

Think about your own experience:

- When did you say yes while everything inside you said no? What happened afterwards?
- When did you say what you thought and it went better than you expected, or worse? What was the difference?
- How do you react when someone tells you that they disagree with you?
