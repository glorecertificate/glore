---
title: Self-assessment
evaluations:
  - I say what I think and what I need in a calm and direct way.
  - I listen to other points of view and take them into account even when I disagree.
  - I can say no politely, without long excuses and without feeling guilty.
  - I give and accept criticism, different opinions and advice without taking them personally.
assessment: Overall, how would you rate your level in assertiveness?
---

Remember a recent moment when you had to speak up, say no, or hear something you did not want to hear. It could be at your volunteering project, in class or at work. What did you say or do? How did the other person react, and how did you feel afterwards?

Keep that moment in mind while you read each statement and rate yourself honestly.
