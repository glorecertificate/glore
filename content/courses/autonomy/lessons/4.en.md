---
title: Self-assessment
evaluations:
  - I start my tasks without waiting to be told, and I look for what needs doing next.
  - I can carry out a task on my own once I know the goal and the limits.
  - I look for answers myself first, and I ask for help when a decision goes beyond my role.
  - I keep track of my own progress and tell others how it is going, so they do not need to follow up.
assessment: Overall, how would you rate your level in autonomy?
---

Remember a recent time when you worked on something with little supervision, at a volunteering project, in your studies or at work. What did you decide by yourself? When did you ask for advice, and was that the right moment?

Keep that example in mind while you read each statement and rate yourself honestly.
