---
title: Self-assessment
evaluations:
  - I notice what people say and how they say it, including tone, posture and silence.
  - I ask questions that help me understand a situation better.
  - I answer questions clearly and say so when I do not know.
  - I give feedback that is specific and kind, and I listen to feedback without getting defensive.
assessment: Overall, how would you rate your level in communication and active listening?
---

Think of one real conversation from your volunteering, studies or work where listening made a difference. It can be one where you understood someone better than expected, or one that went wrong because you missed something. What did you hear, see or ask? What would you do differently?

Keep that example in mind while you read each statement and rate yourself honestly.
