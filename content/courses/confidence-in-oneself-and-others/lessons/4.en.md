title: Self-assessment
evaluations:
  - I can name what I do well and rely on it when a task feels hard.
  - I take on challenges I am not sure I can handle, and I learn from how they go.
  - I trust other people with real responsibility and let them do it their way.
  - I keep my promises and I speak up honestly when I make a mistake.
assessment: Overall, how would you rate your level in confidence in oneself and others?
---

Remember a recent moment when you had to trust yourself or someone else, for example before a new task, after a mistake, or when handing over responsibility. What did you do, and how did it turn out?

Keep that moment in mind while you read each statement and rate yourself honestly.
