---
title: Sizes or types
---

Nikolai and Yasmin volunteer on Tuesdays at a clothes store for families in a refugee support centre in Hamburg. This week they are reorganising the donation room, and they disagree. Nikolai wants everything sorted by size, so that parents can find what fits their children in a minute. Yasmin wants it sorted by type, because volunteers keep putting jackets in the wrong boxes.

At the team meeting, Nikolai says that Yasmin's idea "makes no sense." Yasmin crosses her arms and answers that he never listens. The coordinator, Greta, notices that nobody is talking about the room any more, only about each other.

Greta suggests a ten-minute break. Outside, Yasmin breathes for a moment and notices she is more upset by the tone than by the idea. When they sit down again, she starts with a question: "What is the problem you most want to fix?" Nikolai says that last week a mother waited twenty minutes for school trousers in size 8. Yasmin explains that last week three coats ended up in the shoe box.

Within half an hour they have a plan. The room is sorted by type, and every box has a coloured label for size.

Think about your own experience:

- Think of a recent disagreement. What did you feel in your body, and what did you do with that feeling?
- When have you argued for a solution without asking what the other person needed?
- Think of a conflict that ended well for both sides. What made that possible?
