---
title: Self-assessment
evaluations:
  - I notice my own emotions during a disagreement and calm down before I answer.
  - I ask what matters most to the other person instead of arguing only for my own position.
  - I talk about what happened and how it affects me, without blaming the person.
  - I look for solutions that work for everyone, and I ask for help when a conflict involves harassment or safety.
assessment: Overall, how would you rate your level in conflict management?
---

Remember a recent disagreement at your volunteering project, in class, at work or at home. What did you feel? What did you say or do, and how did it end?

Keep that moment in mind while you read each statement and rate yourself honestly.
