---
title: The bags that kept tearing
---

Mateusz volunteers with a river clean-up group in Kraków. Every Saturday the team collects bottles, cans and packaging from the bank, and every Saturday the same thing happens: the cheap bin bags tear on broken glass, and half the rubbish ends up back on the path.

For weeks the team has repeated the same fix. Use two bags, carry them carefully, complain about the bags. This Saturday Mateusz stops asking how to carry the bags better and asks a different question: why are we putting glass in bags at all?

He remembers that the café near the bridge throws away plastic crates every week. He walks over and asks if the team can borrow a few. Back at the river, Agnieszka suggests labelling one crate for glass and another for cans. Tomasz adds that the crates could stay at the bridge between clean-ups, so people walking by can drop in what they find.

Not every idea works. The first crates are too heavy to carry when they are full, and the team has to try a smaller size. By the third Saturday, not one bag has torn.

Think about your own experience:

- When did you find a different way to solve a problem that everyone else handled the usual way? What started it?
- What is one routine in your volunteering, study or work that has never been questioned? What might you try instead?
- What stops you from sharing an unusual idea with a group?
