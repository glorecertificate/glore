---
title: Self-assessment
evaluations:
  - I come up with several possible solutions to a problem before I choose one.
  - I question rules and assumptions that limit how a problem can be solved.
  - I notice what could be improved in how my group does things and suggest a change.
  - I try a new idea on a small scale and use what I learn to adjust it.
assessment: Overall, how would you rate your level in creativity and innovation?
---

Remember a recent time when you or your group were stuck on a problem, or when something kept being done the same way even though it was not working well. What did you try? How did you come up with it, and what happened?

Keep that moment in mind while you read each statement and rate yourself honestly.
