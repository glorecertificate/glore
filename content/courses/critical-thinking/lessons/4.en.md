---
title: Self-assessment
evaluations:
  - I check who said something, what evidence they give and whether other sources agree before I believe or share it.
  - I form my own opinion after looking at the facts, even when the group or an authority thinks differently.
  - I break a situation into parts and look for several possible causes before I decide.
  - I look for views different from mine and try to understand them before I judge.
assessment: Overall, how would you rate your level in critical thinking?
---

Remember a recent moment when you had to decide what to believe or how to understand a situation. It could be at your volunteering project, in class or at work. What did you ask yourself? Did you change your mind, and why?

Keep that moment in mind while you read each statement and rate yourself honestly.
