---
title: Self-assessment
evaluations:
  - I list the options I have, including doing nothing, and compare what each would cost or bring.
  - I choose with the information I have instead of waiting until I am completely sure.
  - I decide early enough for the decision to still be useful to others.
  - I take responsibility for my choices, explain them to the people affected, and learn from the result.
assessment: Overall, how would you rate your level in decision making?
---

Remember a recent decision you had to make, at your volunteering project, in class, at work or at home. What options did you see? How long did it take you, and how did it turn out?

Keep that decision in mind while you read each statement and rate yourself honestly.
