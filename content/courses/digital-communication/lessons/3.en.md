---
title: Practice
questions:
  - question: You need to tell 25 volunteers that tomorrow's beach clean-up is cancelled because of a storm warning. Which channel is the best choice?
    options:
      - text: Post it on your personal social media story.
      - text: Wait to tell people when they arrive.
      - text: Send it in the group chat and by email, with a clear first line, and ask people to confirm that they have seen it.
        correct: true
    explanation: A cancellation is urgent and everyone must see it. Using two channels and asking for confirmation reduces the chance that someone misses it. A personal story reaches only some followers, and telling people at the site is too late.
  - question: A volunteer replies "ok." to your detailed instructions and you cannot tell if they are annoyed. What is the best step?
    options:
      - text: Ask in a friendly way if the instructions were clear, or call them if the topic is sensitive.
        correct: true
      - text: Assume they are angry and stop asking them to help.
      - text: Write the same instructions again in capital letters.
    explanation: Short written replies are easy to misread. A friendly check, or a short call for something delicate, brings back the tone that a screen takes away. Assuming the worst or writing in capital letters, which reads as shouting, makes the situation worse.
  - question: Your association asks you to run an online info session using a video platform you have never used. What do you do first?
    options:
      - text: Refuse, because you do not know the tool.
      - text: Join a test call with a friend, learn how to share your screen and mute people, and write a short guide for the participants.
        correct: true
      - text: Learn it live during the session, since it cannot be that hard.
    explanation: Familiarity comes from practice in a low-risk setting. A test call lets you find problems before the audience does, and a short guide helps participants who are nervous about the tool too. Learning live risks the whole session.
  - question: You want to share a spreadsheet with participants' phone numbers with another volunteer. What is the safest approach?
    options:
      - text: Post it in the large group chat so everyone has it.
      - text: Send it from a public computer at the library.
      - text: Share only the needed columns, directly with that volunteer, through a link with limited access.
        correct: true
    explanation: Personal data should reach only the people who need it. Sharing the minimum with a person, using limited access, keeps the participants' privacy. Posting it widely or using a public computer leaves copies that you cannot control.
