---
title: Self-assessment
evaluations:
  - I write digital messages that are clear, polite and complete.
  - I choose the right channel for each message and I do not waste other people's time.
  - I take part in online meetings and group chats confidently.
  - I am willing to try new digital tools and I know how to find out how they work.
assessment: Overall, how would you rate your level in digital communication?
---

Remember a recent situation in which you used digital tools to work with others. It might be a group chat for a project, an online class, an email to a coordinator or a video call with a team. What went well? Where did something get lost, delayed or misunderstood?

Keep that example in mind while you read each statement and rate yourself honestly.
