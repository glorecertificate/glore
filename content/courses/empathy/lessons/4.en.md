---
title: Self-assessment
evaluations:
  - I notice how other people are feeling, even when they do not say it.
  - I try to picture a situation from the other person's side before I react or judge.
  - I can show that I care about what someone is going through without taking over their problem.
  - I check whether I have understood someone correctly instead of assuming I know how they feel.
assessment: Overall, how would you rate your level in empathy?
---

Remember a recent moment when someone near you was going through something difficult. It could be at your volunteering project, in class or at work. What did you notice? What did you say or do, and how did the person react?

Keep that moment in mind while you read each statement and rate yourself honestly.
