---
title: Self-assessment
evaluations:
  - I stay calm and think clearly when a plan changes at short notice.
  - I observe a new place or group to understand what is expected before I act.
  - I change my approach when the first one is not working.
  - I keep the goal in mind while I change the way I reach it.
assessment: Overall, how would you rate your level in flexibility?
---

Remember a recent time when something changed unexpectedly, or when you were in a new place with unfamiliar rules. What did you do? What worked, and what would you do differently now?

Keep that moment in mind while you read each statement and rate yourself honestly.
