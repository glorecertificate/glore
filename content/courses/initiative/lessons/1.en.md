---
title: The table by the window
---

Elias volunteers on Friday evenings at a youth centre in Ghent. The games night is busy, but he keeps seeing the same thing: the teenagers who arrived recently sit alone by the window while the others fill the big tables. Nobody has asked him to look into it. His task is to hand out the board games and tidy up.

For two weeks he tells himself that someone with more experience will deal with it. On the third Friday he decides to try something small. He sets up a table near the window with a card game that needs no common language, and he writes "Join in, we will teach you" on a sheet of paper. Two teenagers sit down. By the end of the evening there are five.

On Monday he tells the coordinator, Lotte, what he did and asks if he can keep the table every week and if a second volunteer can help. Lotte says yes, and adds that he should have told her sooner so she could ask the others to welcome the newcomers too. Elias writes that down for next time.

Think about your own experience:

- Think of a time you noticed something that needed doing and nobody had asked you. What did you do?
- When did you hold back an idea because it was not your job? What happened to it?
- When is it better to ask first, and when is it better to start and tell people afterwards?
