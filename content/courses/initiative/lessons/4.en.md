---
title: Self-assessment
evaluations:
  - I notice things that need doing or improving, even when nobody has asked me to handle them.
  - I take a first small step on an idea instead of waiting for permission or for the perfect plan.
  - I check with the right people first when my idea affects others, uses resources or involves sensitive matters.
  - I follow my ideas through, ask for feedback and adjust when something does not work.
assessment: Overall, how would you rate your level in initiative?
---

Remember a time you started something without being asked, at your volunteering project, in class or at work. What did you notice? What was your first step, and what happened next?

Keep that moment in mind while you read each statement and rate yourself honestly.
