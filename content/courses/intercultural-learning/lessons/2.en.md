---
title: 'Working well across cultures'
---

Mixed groups are where most volunteering happens. A few habits make them work.

## Habits that help

- Slow down. Misunderstandings often come from speed. Repeat what you heard in your own words.
- Use plain language. Avoid idioms and jokes that need local knowledge. Speak clearly, and do not speak louder.
- Ask open questions. "How do you usually do this?" teaches you more than "Why do you do it that way?", which can sound like a complaint.
- Share the floor. Some people speak up quickly, others wait to be invited. Invite quieter voices without putting them on the spot.
- Watch for stereotypes. A generalization can be a starting question, but never a conclusion about a person.

## When it goes wrong

You will sometimes offend someone without meaning to. Apologize for the effect, not only for the intention, and ask what to do differently. Then keep going.

## Think about it

- Which habit on the list would help most in the group you work with now?
- Whose voice is hardest to hear in your group, and what could you change?
