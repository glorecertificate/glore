---
title: Painting the youth centre
---

Radek is twenty-three and has volunteered for two years at a youth centre in Kraków. The centre has a grey, damaged common room, and he proposes painting it. The coordinator says yes, as long as he organises it. Fourteen volunteers sign up for Saturday.

At nine o'clock, people stand around the paint cans, unsure what to do. Radek notices that some are on their phones and a few are looking at the door. He gets everyone together and explains the aim: "By tonight, this room should be a place where we are happy to bring the kids on Monday." Then he asks what people want to do. Nour, a quiet student, says that she likes detailed work. Bartek says he has painted before. Radek asks Bartek to show a short technique, and Nour takes charge of the window frames.

At noon, the mood drops. It is hotter than expected, and the second coat is slow. Radek brings cold drinks and puts on a playlist that people have suggested. He also points to the wall that was grey that morning. By late afternoon, the group has finished, and several people ask when the next project is.

Think about your own experience:

- Think of a leader who made you want to take part. What did they do?
- When have you led something, even in a small way? What was easy and what was hard?
- What do you need from a leader in order to give your best?
