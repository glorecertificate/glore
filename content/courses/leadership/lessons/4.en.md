---
title: Self-assessment
evaluations:
  - I help a group see the goal it is working toward and why it matters.
  - I encourage people by recognising their effort and showing what we have achieved together.
  - I help turn a goal into clear tasks that match what each person can and wants to do.
  - I make room for every member to speak and take part, including the quieter ones.
assessment: Overall, how would you rate your level in leadership?
---

Remember a recent moment when you helped a group move forward, or when someone else did. It could be at your volunteering project, in class or at work. What did the person do? How did the group respond?

Keep that moment in mind while you read each statement and rate yourself honestly.
