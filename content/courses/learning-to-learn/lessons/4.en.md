---
title: Self-assessment
evaluations:
  - I know which ways of learning work best for me and I use them.
  - I can state what I want to learn and why it matters to me.
  - I turn a learning aim into concrete steps with a time and a resource.
  - I review my progress and change my plan when it is not working.
assessment: Overall, how would you rate your level in learning to learn?
---

Remember something you recently learned or tried to learn, big or small. How did you go about it? What helped, what got in the way, and what would you change next time?

Keep that example in mind while you read each statement and rate yourself honestly.
