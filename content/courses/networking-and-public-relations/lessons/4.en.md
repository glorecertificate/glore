---
title: Self-assessment
evaluations:
  - I can introduce myself and my organisation in a short and clear way.
  - I start conversations with people and institutions outside my own group.
  - I follow up after a first meeting and offer something useful, not only requests.
  - I keep my contacts active over time, so that they become productive working relationships.
assessment: Overall, how would you rate your level in networking and public relations?
---

Remember a time when you got in touch with someone outside your usual group, or when a contact helped you or your organisation. Maybe at a volunteering event, a school fair or an online community. How did the contact begin? Did it last, and why?

Keep that example in mind while you read each statement and rate yourself honestly.
