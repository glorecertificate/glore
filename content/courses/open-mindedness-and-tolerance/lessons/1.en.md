---
title: A menu for twelve
---

Ioana is on an Erasmus+ youth exchange in Tampere, with twelve participants from seven countries. On the third evening the group has to plan a shared dinner. Sami says he eats no meat. Ayşe is fasting for religious reasons and will eat only after sunset. Another participant, Jonas, asks if there will be pasta, because he does not like trying new food.

Ioana's first thought is that this will be complicated. She had planned a dish with sausage, and she notices she feels slightly irritated. She also notices that the irritation is about her plan, not about the people.

She puts the pen down and asks, "Can you tell me more about what works for you? I would like to cook something we can all share." Ayşe explains how the fast works and suggests moving dinner a little later. Sami mentions two dishes from his home that are easy to make. Jonas agrees to try one new thing, and a plain option stays on the table for him.

The dinner is at nine, with a soup, a big pasta dish, and a tray of roasted vegetables. Ioana saves her sausage dish for another day. Jonas, who planned to skip the vegetables, goes back for seconds.

Think about your own experience:

- Think of a time you met a belief or custom that was new to you. What was your first reaction?
- When did someone ask you about a part of your life with real curiosity? How did it feel?
- Is there an opinion that you find hard to listen to? What makes it hard?
