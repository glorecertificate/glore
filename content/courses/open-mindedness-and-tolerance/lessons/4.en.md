---
title: Self-assessment
evaluations:
  - I notice my first reaction to something unfamiliar and pause before I judge it.
  - I ask respectful questions to understand someone else's ideas, beliefs or ways of living.
  - I listen to opinions I disagree with and try to understand the reasons behind them.
  - I check my assumptions about groups of people, and I speak up when behaviour harms or demeans others.
assessment: Overall, how would you rate your level in open-mindedness and tolerance?
---

Remember a recent moment when you met an idea, a custom or a person that was very different from what you are used to. It can be at your volunteering project, in class or at work. What did you feel? What did you do, and what did you learn?

Keep that moment in mind while you read each statement and rate yourself honestly.
