---
title: Self-assessment
evaluations:
  - I decide what matters most and plan the steps ahead before I start a task.
  - I meet my deadlines, or I warn people early when I am going to miss one.
  - I check the people, money and time I really have before I plan or promise something.
  - I share tasks and responsibilities fairly, taking into account what each person can give.
assessment: Overall, how would you rate your level in organization and time management?
---

Remember a recent task or event you had to organize. It could be a volunteering activity, a project at school or university, or something at work. How did you decide what to do first? Did you finish on time, and who did the work?

Keep that example in mind while you read each statement and rate yourself honestly.
