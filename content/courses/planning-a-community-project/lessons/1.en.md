---
title: 'Start with the need'
---

Joana lives near a small park in Braga that few people use after school. She has a big idea: a weekly games afternoon for children. Before she buys anything, she spends a week talking to parents, a primary school teacher and two older neighbours.

She learns that parents work until six, that some children have nowhere to go, and that neighbours worry about noise. Her idea changes: a quiet activity hour from five to six, with a signed-up adult at every table.

## Why listening first

A project that answers a real need gets support. A project that answers only your own idea risks being ignored, or worse, being a burden for others. Talk to the people you want to help, and to the people who will be affected.

## Questions to ask

- What is difficult here today, and for whom?
- What has already been tried?
- Who else is working on this? Could we join them?

## Think about it

- Think of a need in your neighbourhood. Who could you ask about it this week?
