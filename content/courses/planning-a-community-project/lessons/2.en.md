---
title: 'From idea to plan'
---

A plan does not need to be long. One page with these parts is enough to start.

## The parts of a simple plan

1. Goal: one sentence that says what will change and for whom. "Ten children from our street do their homework in a calm place on Tuesdays" is clearer than "help children".
2. Team: who does what, and who decides when you disagree.
3. Steps: the tasks in order, each with a person and a date.
4. Resources: what you need, what you already have and who can lend it.
5. Risks: what could go wrong, such as bad weather or fewer volunteers, and your plan B.
6. Safety and permission: who must agree (the owner of the place, parents, your organization), and how you keep participants safe.

## Keep it small

Start with a pilot: one afternoon, one group. A small project you finish teaches more than a big one you abandon.

## Look back

At the end, ask the team and the participants three things: what worked, what did not, and what you would change. Write it down. That is how a good project becomes a better one, and it gives you real examples for a GloRe certificate later.

## Think about it

- Write the goal of your idea in one sentence.
