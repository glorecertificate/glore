---
title: The broken bicycle pump
---

Maja runs a bicycle repair workshop for teenagers at a community centre in Graz. On Thursday afternoon ten young people are waiting with flat tyres, and the only pump has just snapped at the handle. The bike shop on the corner closed an hour ago.

Her first thought is to cancel the session. She stops and asks herself what the real problem is. The pump is only a tool: what the group needs is air in ten tyres. She puts the question to the teenagers. "What do we have that can push air into a tyre?"

Ideas come quickly. Luka remembers that the football in the cupboard has a small pump with a needle. Amira says that the café across the street has a compressor for its bicycle delivery. Maja thinks about which option is fastest and costs nothing: the needle pump works on one tyre in a few minutes, and with ten tyres it would take a long time. She tries it on one bike to see. It works but is slow, so she sends Luka and Amira to ask the café, while the others start fixing the inner tubes.

The café owner agrees to let them use the compressor for twenty minutes. By six o'clock all the bikes are rolling, and the next week Maja puts a spare pump in the toolbox.

Think about your own experience:

- Think of a time when something broke or went wrong at the last moment. What did you do first?
- When did a solution come from someone else in the group? How did you ask for it?
- What was the last problem you solved in a way that you had not tried before?
