---
title: What problem solving means
---

This is the GloRe definition:

> Ability to find innovative or efficient solutions to challenges.

Every project has challenges: a plan that falls through, a conflict over resources, a tool that stops working. Problem solving is what you do between "this is a problem" and "this is fixed". The definition names two qualities of a good solution. It can be innovative, meaning new or creative, and it can be efficient, meaning it uses little time, money and effort.

## Understanding the challenge

A solution has to fit the problem, so start there. Before you act, describe the problem in a sentence: what is happening, who is affected and what do you want instead? "The projector is broken" is a fact. "We need to show a film to twenty-five people in twenty minutes" is the problem to solve, and it opens more options than repairing the projector.

At a youth centre, volunteers kept arriving late on Saturdays. The first idea was to remind them more often. When the coordinator asked, she learned that the bus on Saturdays starts an hour later. The problem was the timetable, and moving the start by thirty minutes solved it.

## Finding innovative options

Innovation here can be small: it means looking for options you did not think of first. Ask other people, because someone else has often seen the problem before. Look at what you already have: a person with a useful skill, a free room, an old tool. Change the angle: if you cannot get more money, can you need less?

An online campaign had no budget for ads. The team asked local schools and sports clubs to share the campaign in their group chats and on noticeboards. It reached more people than a small advertising budget would have.

## Choosing efficient solutions

Once you have a few options, compare them. Ask how much time each takes, what it costs, who must be involved and what could go wrong. The best option is the one that fits your situation, and sometimes that is the simple one.

A student association must repaint a common room. Painting it with volunteers costs 100 euros and three weekends. Hiring a painter costs 500 euros and one day. The budget is 150 euros and the room is needed in two months, so the volunteer option fits.

## Trying and adjusting

A solution is a guess until you test it. Try the option on a small scale, check whether it worked and change it if not. When something fails, treat it as information about the problem. A team at work tried a new shared calendar to avoid double bookings, and after two weeks people still overlapped. They found that nobody had been told to update it from their phones, wrote a short guide and the overlaps stopped.
