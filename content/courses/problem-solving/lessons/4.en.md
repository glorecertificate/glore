---
title: Self-assessment
evaluations:
  - I take time to understand a problem before I look for a solution.
  - I come up with more than one possible solution, including new ideas and ones that use what I already have.
  - I compare options by time, cost and effort, and choose one that fits the situation.
  - I try a solution, check whether it worked, and change it if it did not.
assessment: Overall, how would you rate your level in problem solving?
---

Remember a recent problem you solved, at a volunteering project, in your studies or at work. How did you describe the problem? Which options did you consider, and how did you choose one?

Keep that example in mind while you read each statement and rate yourself honestly.
