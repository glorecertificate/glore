---
title: Self-assessment
evaluations:
  - I can prepare a short talk with a clear main message.
  - I can adapt my words and examples to the people who are listening.
  - I speak clearly in front of a group, using my voice and body to support my message.
  - I feel able to speak in front of both people I know and people I do not know, and I can answer their questions.
assessment: Overall, how would you rate your level in public speaking?
---

Think of one time you spoke in front of others. It might have been a presentation at school, a welcome at a volunteer event, a toast, or a short answer in a meeting. What did you say? How did the audience react? What helped you, and what would you change?

Hold on to that example while you read each statement and rate yourself honestly.
