---
title: Self-assessment
evaluations:
  - I do what I have promised to do, or I tell people early when I cannot.
  - I say clearly what I did, including when something went wrong.
  - I look for ways to repair the effects of my mistakes instead of looking for excuses.
  - I separate what is under my control from what is not, and I act on the part that is mine.
assessment: Overall, how would you rate your level in responsibility?
---

Remember a recent moment when you made a commitment, or when something went wrong because of you. It could be at your volunteering project, in class or at work. What did you do? How did the others react, and how did you feel afterwards?

Keep that moment in mind while you read each statement and rate yourself honestly.
