---
title: Self-assessment
evaluations:
  - I can name several things I do well and several things I find difficult, with examples.
  - I pay attention to my reactions and to other people's feedback to understand myself better.
  - I take time after a good or a hard experience to ask what I learned.
  - I choose small, specific steps to improve something about myself and check how they work.
assessment: Overall, how would you rate your level in self-awareness?
---

Remember a recent moment when you noticed something about yourself, good or difficult. It could be at your volunteering project, in class or at work. What did you notice? What did you do with it afterwards?

Keep that moment in mind while you read each statement and rate yourself honestly.
