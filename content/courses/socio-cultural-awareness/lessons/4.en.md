---
title: Self-assessment
evaluations:
  - I notice how my own background shapes what feels normal to me.
  - I ask about people's customs and needs instead of guessing from stereotypes.
  - I adapt how I communicate and work when people have different norms from mine.
  - I check that people who are new or on the margins can take part fully in a group.
assessment: Overall, how would you rate your level in socio-cultural and multicultural awareness?
---

Remember a recent time when you were in a group with people from different backgrounds, or when you were the one who felt different. What did you notice? What did you do, or what did others do, to help everyone feel part of it?

Keep that moment in mind while you read each statement and rate yourself honestly.
