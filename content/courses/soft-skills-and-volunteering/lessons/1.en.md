---
title: 'Skills you already use'
---

Maya helps run a weekend sports club for teenagers in Gdańsk. One Saturday the referee does not show up, two teams argue about the score, and a younger player starts to cry. Within ten minutes Maya has found a replacement, calmed the argument and sat down next to the player.

She would not call that a skill. She would say it was just a hard Saturday. But she planned under pressure, listened to both sides and noticed how someone felt. Those are soft skills.

Soft skills are the abilities that help you work and live with other people, such as communication, teamwork, problem-solving or empathy. Unlike a technical skill, like using a spreadsheet, they show up in almost every setting.

## Think about it

- Describe a moment in your volunteering when something went wrong and you handled it.
- What did you do, step by step?
- Which of those steps could you do in another place, such as a job or a class?
