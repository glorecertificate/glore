Learning does not only happen in classrooms. Non-formal learning is what you pick up in organized activities that are not school, such as a youth project or a volunteering placement. Informal learning is what happens in daily life, without a plan. Volunteering mixes both.

## Why it works

- You work with people you did not choose, so you practise teamwork and communication.
- Real people depend on what you do, so you practise responsibility.
- Plans change, so you practise flexibility and problem-solving.
- You meet people from other backgrounds, so you practise open-mindedness.

## The missing step

Experience alone does not always turn into a skill. The step that helps is reflection: stopping to ask what happened, what you did, and what you would repeat or change. Without it, a great experience can stay a nice memory.

GloRe asks you to do exactly this. Each skill course helps you name a skill, then asks you to rate yourself honestly against real examples.
