---
title: Self-assessment
evaluations:
  - I notice the signs of stress in my body and thoughts early.
  - I can pause and calm myself before I react in a tense moment.
  - I use ways of coping that work for me, such as planning, breaks, movement or talking, when the pressure grows.
  - I ask for support from a coordinator, a colleague or a professional when the stress is too much to handle alone.
assessment: Overall, how would you rate your level in stress management?
---

Remember a recent stressful moment, at a volunteering project, in your studies or at work. What did you feel, and what did you do? What helped, and what would you try next time?

Keep that example in mind while you read each statement and rate yourself honestly.
