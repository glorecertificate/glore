---
title: Lunch for eighty people
---

On Saturdays, Tomasz joins a team of five that cooks lunch at a community kitchen in Gdańsk. Today there are eighty portions to serve by half past twelve. Lena runs the stove, Yusuf prepares the vegetables, Ania sets the tables, and Tomasz washes dishes and stacks plates.

At noon the soup catches on the bottom of the pot. Lena says nothing, but her shoulders go stiff and she starts stirring faster. Tomasz sees it from the sink. He finishes the pan in his hands, dries them and walks over. "What do you need?" he asks. Lena says she needs someone to start a second pot of rice while she saves the soup. Yusuf has finished his vegetables, so he takes the rice. Ania moves the first tables to the serving line and tells the guests there will be a ten-minute wait.

Nobody gives orders. Each person does the part that is in front of them and looks up now and then to see where the team needs them. The food goes out at ten to one. Afterwards the five of them sit down with the leftover rice, and Lena says that next time she will ask for help sooner.

Think about your own experience:

- Think of a team you enjoyed being part of. What did the people in it do that made it work?
- When did you notice that a teammate needed help? Did you offer, and what happened?
- What is your usual role in a group (leading, supporting, organizing, joking)? Is there a role you avoid?
