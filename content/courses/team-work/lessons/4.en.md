---
title: Self-assessment
evaluations:
  - I do my part of a shared task reliably, and the team can count on what I promise.
  - I listen to my teammates and share my ideas in a way that helps the group decide.
  - I offer help when someone is overloaded, and I ask for help when I need it.
  - I support a group decision and work to make it succeed, even when I suggested something else.
assessment: Overall, how would you rate your level in team work?
---

Remember a recent time you worked with others, for example at a volunteering project, in a class group or at work. What was your part? How did the team decide things, and what did you do when something went wrong?

Keep that example in mind while you read each statement and rate yourself honestly.
