---
title: 'When something is not safe'
---

Sometimes the problem is not tiredness but a situation. Someone makes you uncomfortable, a group is pressured to do something risky, a young person tells you something worrying, or a place does not feel safe.

## The first step

Speak to your coordinator or to your organization's safeguarding contact. Do this first, as soon as you can, for anything that feels unsafe, whether it affects you, another volunteer or a person you work with. Do not wait until you are sure, and do not try to handle it alone.

If someone is in immediate danger, call the local emergency number first, then tell your coordinator.

## What you can say

Short and factual is enough: what happened, when, where, who was there. You do not need proof. If someone confides in you, listen, do not promise to keep it secret, and tell them you will pass it to the person whose job is to help.

## What this course does not do

It does not give medical or legal advice. For those, your organization can point you to qualified people and services.

## Think about it

- Who is the safeguarding contact or coordinator in your organization? If you do not know yet, find out this week.
