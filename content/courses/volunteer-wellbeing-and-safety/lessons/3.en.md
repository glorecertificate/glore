      - text: 'Take more shifts to prove your commitment'
      - text: 'Say nothing and hope it passes'
    explanation: 'Limits keep you able to help for longer. Telling your coordinator early lets them plan, while silence and extra shifts usually make exhaustion worse.'
  - question: 'A fellow volunteer says she has had trouble sleeping and feels overwhelmed. How can you support her?'

      - text: 'Listen, and encourage her to talk to the coordinator'
        correct: true
      - text: 'Take over all her tasks indefinitely'
    explanation: 'Listening shows you care, and pointing to the coordinator connects her with people who can change her load. Taking everything over is not sustainable for you.'

Read each situation and choose the response that protects you and the people around you. If you are unsure in real life, speak to your coordinator first.
