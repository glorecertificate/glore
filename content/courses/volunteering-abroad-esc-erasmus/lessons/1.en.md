---
title: 'Two European programmes'
---

The European Union supports young people who want to learn and help abroad. Two programmes come up most often in volunteering.

## The European Solidarity Corps

The European Solidarity Corps (ESC) lets young people take part in solidarity projects that help communities. Many people know it for volunteering placements: you join an organization in your own country or abroad and work on a project, for example with refugees, in an environmental project or in a youth centre.

## Erasmus+

Erasmus+ is the EU programme for education, training, youth and sport. For young people outside formal education, it supports activities such as youth exchanges, where groups from different countries meet to work on a theme together, and training for youth workers.

## How they relate

Both programmes are about mobility and learning outside school. ESC focuses on solidarity and volunteering. Erasmus+ is wider and covers study, training, youth and sport. Rules, ages, durations and funding change from one call to the next, so never rely on a friend's story: check the official sources.
