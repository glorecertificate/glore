---
title: 'Before you say yes'
---

Going abroad as a volunteer is an agreement between you, a sending organization, a hosting organization and sometimes others. Before you commit, get answers in writing or in a clear conversation.

## Questions to ask

- What exactly will I do each week, and who will support me?
- Who is my contact person, and who can I call if something goes wrong?
- What does the project cover, and what do I pay myself?
- Where will I live, and with whom?
- How long is the project, and what happens if I need to leave early?
- What language will we use at work?

## Look at the organization

Find the organization's website and its past projects. Ask to speak with someone who volunteered there before. A good host answers openly and does not rush you.

## Prepare yourself

Tell people close to you your plan and dates. Learn a few words of the local language. Think about what you want to learn, because it will help you describe the experience later in a GloRe certificate.

## Think about it

- Which question on the list would you be least comfortable asking, and why?
