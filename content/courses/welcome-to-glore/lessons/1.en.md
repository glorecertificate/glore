---
title: 'What GloRe is for'
---

GloRe, short for Global Recognition, helps you turn volunteering into something you can show. Many people learn a lot while they help at a festival, a youth centre or a refugee support project, but a CV or a school application rarely has a place for it.

GloRe gives that learning a name. You follow short courses on soft skills, such as teamwork or empathy, and you connect them to a real volunteering experience. At the end you can ask for a certificate that employers and schools can check for themselves.

## Who is behind it

The platform is run by Associazione Joint, a non-profit in Milan that works on youth mobility, European volunteering and non-formal education. GloRe grew into a network of organizations that issue certificates under shared quality standards.

## Think about it

- What have you learned from volunteering that you could not easily put in a CV?
- Who would you like to show it to?
