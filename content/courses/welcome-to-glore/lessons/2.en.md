---
title: 'Your path to a certificate'
---

Volunteers join GloRe through an organization, never alone. Your organization invites you or approves your request, and your role decides what you can do. Volunteers take skill courses and can request certificates. Learners take introductory and learner courses only.

## The steps

1. Take skill courses. Each one ends with a self-assessment where you rate your own level.
2. Complete at least three skill courses with an average rating of 3 out of 5 or more.
3. Describe your volunteering experience: where, when, how long and what you did.
4. Send the certificate request. It goes to a tutor in your organization.
5. The tutor reviews it. They can ask for changes, reject it with a reason, or approve it.
6. After approval, the certificate is final. GloRe creates a PDF with a QR code and a public page.

## What makes it trustworthy

A tutor from a real organization has checked your request. Once approved, the certificate cannot be edited, and anyone can scan the QR code to reach its public page and see that it is verified.
