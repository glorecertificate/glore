---
title: Self-assessment
evaluations:
  - I keep up a steady effort on a task, including the dull parts.
  - I do my work with the same care whether or not someone is checking it.
  - I keep my commitments, and when I cannot, I tell people early and offer what I can still do.
  - I say yes only to what I can do well, and I see my tasks through to the end.
assessment: Overall, how would you rate your level in work ethic and commitment?
---

Remember a time when you kept a promise that was hard to keep, or when you had to tell someone you could not. It can be at your volunteering project, in class or at work. What did you do and how did it end?

Keep that moment in mind while you read each statement and rate yourself honestly.
