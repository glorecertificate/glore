---
title: 'How long must my volunteering last?'
excerpt: 'At least 100 hours, or at least 3 months between your start and end date.'
---

Your volunteering has to reach one of two limits:

- at least 100 hours in total, or
- a period of at least 3 months from your start date to your end date.

Meeting one of them is enough. GloRe checks the rule when you submit the request, and the tutor checks it again when they approve it. If your activity is shorter, you can request a certificate later, once it reaches a limit.
