---
title: 'Can I get another certificate later?'
excerpt: 'Yes. Request a new one for a new volunteering period or experience.'
---

You can hold several certificates. Each one covers one period of volunteering. After your first is approved, request another when you have volunteered again, with the same organization or a different one.

You can have one open request per organization at a time, so finish or withdraw the current one before you start another. The skill courses you completed stay completed.
