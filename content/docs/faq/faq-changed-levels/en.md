---
title: 'My tutor changed my levels. Why?'
excerpt: 'The tutor sets the final level of each skill after reading your request and your reflections.'
---

Your self-assessment is where the certificate starts, but the tutor decides the final level of each skill. They can confirm it, raise it or lower it, and they write a short statement about what you showed.

Specific examples in your reflections and in your list of tasks help the tutor see what you did. If a level surprises you, ask your tutor. They know your activity and can explain their choice. When they ask for changes, you can also update your request and send it again.
