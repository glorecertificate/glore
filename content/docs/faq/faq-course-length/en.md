---
title: 'How long does a skill course take?'
excerpt: 'About 35 to 40 minutes. Every skill course has four short lessons.'
---

Each skill course has four lessons: a scenario to think about, the definition of the skill, a practice lesson with a quiz, and a self-assessment. Most take about 35 minutes, and a few take 40.

You can stop after any lesson. The app saves your progress, so Resume course puts you back where you were.
