---
title: 'Who can see my data?'
excerpt: 'You, the people who run your organization and your tutors, and the GloRe team when it needs to. Certificates are public by design.'
---

- You see everything in your account.
- Your organization sees the data it needs to run its activity: your profile basics, your course progress and your certificate requests. This includes the people who run it and its tutors.
- Anyone with the link or code of an approved certificate sees what the certificate page shows.
- The GloRe team can look at user accounts to run the platform and give support. Sensitive actions are recorded in an audit log.

The AI assistant in the course editor is only used on course texts, not on learners' data. For the full picture, read the [privacy policy](/privacy).
