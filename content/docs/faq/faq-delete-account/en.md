---
title: 'How do I delete my account?'
excerpt: 'Open Settings, then Account, and choose Delete account. Two cases need an extra step.'
---

Open Settings, go to Account and choose Delete account. This removes your account, your profile and your progress for good.

Two cases need an extra step:

- If you are the only owner of an organization, transfer ownership to another member first, or delete the organization.
- If you already hold certificates, deleting may not be possible right away. Certificates are public records that your organization issued, so the page may ask you to contact your organization.

The [privacy policy](/privacy) explains your rights over your data.
