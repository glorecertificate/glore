---
title: 'How do I delete my account?'
excerpt: 'Open Settings, then Your data, and choose Delete account. Owners need an extra step.'
---

Open Settings, go to Your data and choose Delete account. This removes your account, your profile, your drafts, your open requests and your progress for good.

The only thing that blocks deletion is being the sole owner of an organization. In that case, open your organization's Settings and, under Danger zone, transfer the ownership to a representative, or delete the organization. Then come back and delete your account.

Certificates that were already issued are kept, with their details and PDF, and stay valid for anyone who verifies them. Their verification page may note that you no longer have an account. To have one revoked, ask your organization or write to us.

The [privacy policy](/privacy) explains your rights over your data.
