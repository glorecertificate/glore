---
title: 'Where can I get help?'
excerpt: 'Ask your organization first. If it cannot help, contact the GloRe team through the Help page.'
---

Start with your organization. Your tutor or the people who run it know your activity and can answer questions about courses, requests and roles.

If your organization cannot help, or the problem is with the platform itself, open [Help](/help) in the sidebar. It shows how to contact the GloRe team.
