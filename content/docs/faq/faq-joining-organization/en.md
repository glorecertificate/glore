---
title: 'How do I join an organization?'
excerpt: 'Through an invitation email or an invite link from the organization.'
---

There are two ways in.

- An invitation. Someone from the organization invites your email address. You get a message with a link that works for 7 days. Open it to create your account, or to accept if you already have one.
- An invite link. The organization shares a link in a group chat or an email. Depending on how it was set up, you join straight away or you send a request that someone approves.

If a link says it has expired or been used up, ask the organization for a new one. You can belong to several organizations and switch between them in the sidebar.
