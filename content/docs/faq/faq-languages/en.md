---
title: 'Which languages can I use?'
excerpt: 'English, Spanish and Italian. You can change the language at any time.'
---

The app is available in English, Spanish and Italian. Change the language in your account settings whenever you like.

Courses are written in all three languages. If a course or lesson is not available in your language yet, you read it in another one and a note tells you which.

You choose the language of your certificate when you request it. It starts with the language you use in the app.
