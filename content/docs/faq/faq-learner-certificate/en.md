---
title: 'I am a learner. Can I get a certificate?'
excerpt: 'Not as a learner. Ask your organization to change your role to volunteer.'
---

Certificates are for volunteers. A learner takes introductory and learner courses and has no access to skill courses or certificate requests.

If you are volunteering with your organization and want a certificate, ask the people who run it to change your role to volunteer.
