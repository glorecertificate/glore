---
title: 'My organization is not part of GloRe. What can I do?'
excerpt: 'Ask it to apply. Volunteers join through a member organization, never on their own.'
---

You cannot sign up alone, because every volunteer belongs to an organization that issues the certificate.

If your organization wants to join the network, someone who represents it can apply on the [registration page](/register). The application asks for the legal representative, a contact person and a motivation, and the organization accepts the Quality Charter. The GloRe team reviews it, and an approved organization gets an invitation for its owner.

If you also volunteer with an organization that is already a member, you can join through that one. For anything else, see [where to get help](/docs/articles/faq-help).
