---
title: 'Is my profile public?'
excerpt: 'No. A public profile is off by default, and you turn it on yourself in Settings.'
---

Your profile is private until you decide otherwise. To make it public, open Settings, then Privacy. You can also choose which certificate to feature.

A certificate is different. Once it is approved, anyone who has its link or code can see the certificate page, which shows your name, the organization, the activity and the skills. Leave out of your description anything you do not want to be public.
