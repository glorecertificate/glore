---
title: 'How long does a review take?'
excerpt: 'It depends on your organization. GloRe does not set a fixed time.'
---

Tutors are people in your organization, with their own schedules, and each organization works at its own pace. GloRe cannot promise a time.

You get a notification and an email whenever the status of your request changes. The people who run your organization can see requests that are waiting and can pass them to another tutor. If it is taking long, ask your tutor or your organization.
