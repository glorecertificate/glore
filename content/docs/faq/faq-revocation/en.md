---
title: 'Can a certificate be revoked?'
excerpt: 'Yes, by the organization or the GloRe team, with a reason. A certificate does not expire otherwise.'
---

A certificate has no expiry date. It stays valid until someone revokes it.

The owner or a representative of the organization, or a GloRe platform admin, can revoke it, for example when the information turns out to be wrong. They must write a reason. After that, the verification page shows the certificate as revoked and gives the date.
