---
title: 'How do I share my certificate?'
excerpt: 'Download the PDF, copy the link, show the QR code or add it to LinkedIn.'
---

Open your approved certificate in Certificates. The share panel gives you:

- the PDF to download,
- a link to copy,
- a QR code,
- a shortcut to add the certificate to your LinkedIn profile.

Anyone with the link or the code can see the certificate and its verification page.
