---
title: 'What do the skill levels mean?'
excerpt: 'There are three levels: Intermediate, Good and Advanced. A certificate shows the level and description a tutor confirmed.'
---

Skills have three levels: Intermediate, Good and Advanced. Each skill course describes the three levels in words.

At the end of each skill course you read three descriptions and choose the one that fits you best. The descriptions do not show their level. Your choice is final and is the starting point of the certificate. The tutor then confirms it or picks another description, and you see the level only when the certificate is issued.
