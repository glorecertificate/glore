---
title: 'What do the skill levels mean?'
excerpt: 'There are five levels, from Absent to Advanced. A certificate shows the level a tutor confirmed.'
---

Skills are rated on five levels: Absent, Basic, Intermediate, Good and Advanced.

You rate yourself at the end of each skill course. Your rating is the starting point of the certificate, and the tutor then confirms the level for each skill. A certificate lists only skills at Basic or higher.
