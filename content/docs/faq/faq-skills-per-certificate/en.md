---
title: 'How many skills does a certificate have?'
excerpt: 'Three or four. You need at least 3 completed skill courses to ask for one.'
---

A certificate lists 3 or 4 skills. To request one you need:

- at least 3 completed skill courses, and
- an average self-assessment of 3 out of 5 or higher across the skill courses you completed.

In the request you choose which 3 or 4 skills go on the certificate. The tutor can remove a skill if your level in it is too low to appear, but a certificate always keeps at least 3.
