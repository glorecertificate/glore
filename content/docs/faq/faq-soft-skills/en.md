---
title: 'What are soft skills?'
excerpt: 'Abilities you use in many situations, such as communicating, working in a team or solving problems.'
---

Soft skills are abilities that are not tied to one job or one subject. You use them when you explain an idea, work with a group, deal with a disagreement or keep going when things get stressful.

GloRe covers 25 of them, in four groups:

- Communication skills, such as communication and active listening, or public speaking.
- Personal skills, such as empathy, self-awareness or leadership.
- Work skills, such as team work, stress management or initiative.
- Social skills, such as flexibility, conflict management or open-mindedness.

Volunteering is a good place to practise them, and each skill has its own course.
