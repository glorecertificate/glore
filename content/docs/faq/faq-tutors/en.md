---
title: 'What do tutors do?'
excerpt: 'Tutors in your organization review certificate requests and decide on them.'
---

A tutor is someone in your organization who reviews certificate requests. When you submit a request, GloRe assigns it to a tutor. They read your request and your course results, check your experience, and set the level and a statement for each skill.

Then they decide. They can approve the request, ask you for changes with comments, or reject it with a reason. Only tutors review requests, and nobody reviews their own.
