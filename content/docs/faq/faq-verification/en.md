---
title: 'How can someone check my certificate?'
excerpt: 'With the code on the PDF or by scanning its QR code.'
---

Every approved certificate has a code made of three groups of four characters, and the PDF has a QR code that leads to the same page.

Anyone can open [the verification page](/verify) and type the code, or scan the QR code with a phone. The page shows whether the certificate is valid or revoked, the holder, the organization that issued it, the period, the hours, the tasks, and the skills with their levels.
