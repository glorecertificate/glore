## Get in

Ask your organization to invite you. You will receive an email with a link that works for 7 days. Open it and create your account.

Your organization may also share an invite link. Open it, and depending on how it was set up you either join straight away or send a request that someone in the organization approves. If you already have an account, sign in first.

## Finish your profile

On your first login GloRe asks for your first and last name, birthday, nationality, birth place, language and a password. A phone number is optional. The certificate needs the personal details, so you only enter them once. At the end GloRe shows your username. You can sign in with your email or your username.

## Look around

- Dashboard shows where you stand and what to do next.
- Courses lists the courses you can take.
- Certificates is where volunteers request and keep their certificates, and where tutors find their queue.
- Organization is for the people who run an organization.
- Documentation holds these guides, and Help tells you how to reach people.
- Settings holds your profile, language and account.
- The bell shows your notifications.

## Take your first course

Open Courses and start Welcome to GloRe, a short introduction that takes about 20 minutes. If you are a volunteer, set up your skill compass next: choose up to 3 strengths and up to 5 goals, and GloRe recommends courses for you.
