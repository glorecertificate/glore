Your role decides what you see and what you can do. You have one role in each organization you belong to, so you can be a volunteer in one and a tutor in another. Use the organization switcher in the sidebar to move between them.

## Roles in an organization

- Volunteer: takes skill courses and requests certificates. Read the [volunteer tutorial](/docs/articles/tutorial-volunteer).
- Learner: takes introductory and learner courses. Learners cannot take skill courses or request certificates. Read the [learner tutorial](/docs/articles/tutorial-learner).
- Tutor: reviews the certificate requests assigned to them. Read the [tutor tutorial](/docs/articles/tutorial-tutor).
- Representative: invites people, approves join requests, assigns tutors and follows the certificates of the organization. Read the [representative tutorial](/docs/articles/tutorial-organization-representative).
- Owner: each organization has exactly one. The owner does everything a representative does, and also changes roles, edits the organization details, transfers ownership and can delete the organization. Read the [owner tutorial](/docs/articles/tutorial-organization-owner).

Only tutors review certificates. Owners and representatives can assign a request to a tutor, but they never review it themselves.

## Roles on the GloRe team

- Editor: writes, translates and publishes courses and documentation. Read the [editor tutorial](/docs/articles/tutorial-editor).
- Platform admin: approves organizations, manages users and the team, looks up certificates and can revoke them. Admins can also edit courses and documentation. Read the [platform admin tutorial](/docs/articles/tutorial-platform-admin).

People on the GloRe team do not see the Organization area.
