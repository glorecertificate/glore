---
title: 'Tutorial for learners'
excerpt: 'Take introductory and learner courses, track your progress, and find out what to do if you want a certificate.'
---

As a learner you explore soft skills and volunteering at your own pace. You do not take skill courses and you do not request certificates.

## Find your courses

1. Sign in. Your dashboard shows the courses you can continue.
2. Open Courses in the sidebar. You see the introductory courses and the learner courses. Skill courses do not appear for learners.
3. Open a course to see its length and what you will learn. Select Start course.

## Work through a course

1. Read each lesson and select Complete and continue.
2. In a quiz lesson, answer every question. After a wrong answer you read the explanation and try again.
3. If the course is not available in your language yet, you read it in another language and a note tells you.
4. At the end, rate the course and add a comment if you like.

## Pick up where you left off

Your progress is saved lesson by lesson. Select Resume course on the course page or on your dashboard.

## If you want a certificate

Certificates are for volunteers. If you are volunteering with your organization and want one, ask the people who run it to change your role to volunteer.

## If you belong to more than one organization

Use the organization switcher in the sidebar. Your role can differ in each organization.
