---
title: 'Tutorial for organization owners'
excerpt: 'Apply for your organization, set it up, and manage roles, certificates and ownership.'
---

Each organization has exactly one owner. You do everything a [representative](/docs/articles/tutorial-organization-representative) does, plus the steps below.

## Apply for your organization

1. Open the [registration page](/register) and fill in the application: the legal representative, registry ID, contact person, website, how many volunteers you work with each year, and your motivation.
2. Accept the Quality Charter.
3. The GloRe team reviews the application. When it is approved, you receive an invitation by email.
4. Accept the invitation, finish your profile, and you land on the dashboard as the owner of the organization.

## Set up the organization

Your Overview shows a checklist: complete the profile and the signatory, invite a tutor, invite volunteers, and get your first certificate.

1. Open the Settings tab of Organization.
2. Fill in the name, legal name, registry ID, contact details and a description in each language.
3. Enter the legal representative who signs your certificates. You can add a signature image if you have one.
4. Upload your logo.

## Manage roles

In the Members tab, change a person's role. Every organization needs a tutor to review certificates, so give that role to someone you trust.

## Revoke a certificate

If a certificate turns out to be wrong, open it from the Certificates tab and revoke it. Write a reason of at least 10 characters. The public page then shows the certificate as revoked.

## Hand over ownership

Open the Members tab and transfer ownership to another member. You confirm by typing the requested text, and both of you receive an email. If you are the only owner, you must do this before you can leave the organization or delete your account.

## Delete the organization

In the Settings tab, the danger zone lets you delete the organization. It only works when the organization has no certificates.
