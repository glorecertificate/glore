---
title: 'Tutorial for organization representatives'
excerpt: 'Invite people, approve join requests, assign tutors, and keep an eye on the certificates of your organization.'
---

Open Organization in the sidebar. Its tabs cover everything you manage. Roles, organization settings, ownership transfers and deleting the organization stay with the owner.

## Invite people

1. Open the Invitations tab and select Invite people. You can also invite from the Members tab.
2. Enter an email address and choose a role: tutor, volunteer or learner. Add a short message if you like. You cannot invite owners or representatives.
3. To invite many people at once, paste up to 50 addresses. The app shows the result for each one.
4. An invitation works for 7 days. In the Invitations tab you can resend it, copy its link or revoke it. A revoked invitation stops working at once.

## Share an invite link

1. In the Invitations tab, select Create link.
2. Choose volunteer or learner, when the link expires, how many people can use it, and whether each person needs your approval.
3. Share the link. You can revoke it at any time. People who already joined keep their access.

## Approve join requests

Open the Join requests tab. Approve or reject each request, and add a short message when you reject one. If someone used an invite link without an account, approving the request sends them an invitation by email.

## Follow certificates

1. Open the Certificates tab to see how many requests sit at each stage.
2. Reassign a request to another tutor when someone is away or overloaded.
3. Make sure your organization has at least one tutor. Without a tutor, requests wait unassigned.

## Keep your members tidy

In the Members tab, search and filter people, and remove someone who left. Removing a member keeps their account and their certificates. The Insights tab shows progress, certificates over time and review times.
