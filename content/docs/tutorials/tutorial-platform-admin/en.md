Platform admins look after the whole platform. You do not see the Organization area, but you can open any organization from the admin pages.

## Overview

Open Admin in the sidebar. The overview shows organizations, members, certificates over time and review times.

## Organizations

1. Open Organizations. New applications wait in the queue.
2. Open an application to read the details and the Quality Charter acceptance.
3. Approve it, and the applicant receives an invitation by email. Or reject it with a comment, and the applicant receives the decision.
4. Open an organization at any time to see its members, invitations, certificates, settings and activity.

## Users

1. Open Users and search or filter the list.
2. Ban a user with a reason and an optional end date. Unban them when it is time.
3. Change a user's platform role.
4. To help someone, impersonate a non-admin user. You must give a reason, a banner stays visible while you act, and the session ends after 15 minutes. Stop impersonating from the banner.

## Team

Open Team to invite admins and editors, resend an invitation, or change a team role.

## Certificates

Open Certificates, search by code, and open a certificate. To revoke one, write a reason of at least 10 characters.

## Audit log

Open Audit to see sensitive actions with who did them and when, including impersonation and revocations.

## Content

Admins can also edit courses and documentation. Use the [editor tutorial](/docs/articles/tutorial-editor) for that.
