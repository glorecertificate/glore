---
title: 'Tutorial for tutors'
excerpt: 'Find the requests waiting for you, review them, and decide: approve, ask for changes, or reject.'
---

Tutors review certificate requests. You are the person who confirms that a certificate matches what the volunteer did.

## Find requests

1. Open Certificates. The queue has tabs for the requests assigned to you, the unassigned requests in your organization, and the ones you completed. A badge shows how long each request has waited.
2. GloRe assigns a new request to the tutor with the fewest open ones. To take an unassigned request yourself, use the unassigned tab.
3. If you cannot review a request, release it so another tutor can take it.
4. You never review your own request.

## Review a request

1. Open the request. It moves to In review and the volunteer gets a notification.
2. Read the request and the volunteer's course results, including their reflections.
3. Check the experience: dates, hours, place and tasks. You can edit what is wrong.
4. For each skill, set the level and write a short statement about what the volunteer showed. You can confirm, raise or lower the level the volunteer gave themselves. A certificate keeps 3 or 4 skills, and a skill at level Absent cannot be on it, so remove that skill instead.
5. Fill in the evaluation scores.

## Decide

- Approve. Preview the PDF first. After you approve, the certificate is final and the volunteer receives it.
- Request changes. Leave a comment on each section that needs work, for example the tasks. The volunteer edits and sends the request back to you.
- Reject. Write a reason of at least 10 characters. The request ends and the volunteer sees your reason.

## Good to know

Owners and representatives can reassign requests, but only tutors review them. Write comments the volunteer can act on. They see exactly what you write.
