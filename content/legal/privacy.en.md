This text explains how GloRe Certificate handles personal data. It is a temporary text and will be replaced by the final policy.

## Who is responsible

The data controller is Associazione Joint, a youth-mobility non-profit in Milan, which runs GloRe Certificate. Contact: info@glorecertificate.net.

## What data we store

- Account data: your name, email address, username, password (stored in protected form) and language.
- Profile data you choose to add: avatar, bio, phone number, birthday, sex, pronouns, city, country and spoken languages.
- Organization data: the organizations you belong to, your role in each, invitations and join requests, and the details an organization enters about itself, such as its name, logo, address and contact information.
- Learning data: your course enrolments, completed lessons, quiz answers, self-assessments and skill ratings.
- Certificate data: the volunteering experience you describe, your activity dates and duration, the personal details a certificate needs (last name, birthday, birth place and nationality), reviewer comments and decisions, and the generated PDF.
- Uploaded files: avatars, organization logos, images added to course content and certificate PDFs.
- Technical data: session information and request counts used to protect the service from abuse.

## Public certificates

An approved certificate is public by design. Its page shows your name, your organization, the activity details, the issue date, a verified badge and a QR code, and the PDF can be downloaded by anyone with the link. Do not include information in a certificate that you do not want to be public.

## Why we use your data and on what basis

- To create and run your account and let you use the courses and certificates you asked for (performance of the service you requested).
- To let organizations and tutors review and issue certificates (performance of the service, and the legitimate interest of organizations in assessing their volunteers).
- To send service emails, such as verification, password reset, invitations and review decisions (performance of the service).
- To keep the platform secure and prevent abuse (legitimate interest).
- To measure how the platform is used with analytics that do not use cookies and do not identify you (legitimate interest).
- To meet legal obligations (legal obligation).

We process your data to provide the service you use (performance of a contract), to keep the platform secure and send service emails (legitimate interest), to meet legal obligations, and, where we ask for it, with your consent.

## Who processes data for us

We use these providers to run the platform:

- Vercel, which hosts the application.
- Neon, which provides the PostgreSQL database.
- Cloudflare R2, which stores uploaded files and certificate PDFs.
- An email service provider, reached over SMTP, which delivers service emails.
- Google Gemini, used only as a writing assistant in the course editor. It receives the text an editor asks it to work on, and it is not used for learners' data.

Organizations you belong to can see the data about you that the platform shows to their members and tutors.

## Transfers outside the European Economic Area

The application servers and the database are configured in the European Union. Some providers listed above may process data outside the European Economic Area. Where this happens we rely on appropriate safeguards, such as the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

## Cookies and analytics

We use only essential cookies: they keep you signed in, remember your language and remember your active organization. They are needed for the service to work, so we do not ask for consent and show no cookie banner. We use analytics that do not use cookies.

## How long we keep data

We keep your data while your account is active. Expired, revoked, or declined invitations are deleted after 30 days, and expired sessions and verification links are deleted every day. You can delete your account in your settings. Approved certificates are public records that an organization has issued, so deleting your account may be subject to additional conditions.

## Your rights

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) you can ask us to give you access to your data, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to its use, and give you a copy in a portable format. Where we rely on your consent, you can withdraw it at any time. You can edit most of your data directly in your settings. For anything else, write to info@glorecertificate.net.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority. In Italy this is the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali. You can also contact the authority in your own country.

## Contact

Questions about this policy: info@glorecertificate.net.

## Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top of the page shows the latest update. If a change is important, we will tell you on the platform or by email.
