## Certificates

A certificate is issued by an organization after a tutor of that organization reviews your request. GloRe provides the platform and does not decide on your behalf. The tutor may ask for changes or reject a request. Once approved, a certificate cannot be edited, and it is publicly visible with a verification page and a QR code. An organization or the GloRe team may revoke a certificate, with a stated reason, for example if the information turns out to be false. Certificates show a volunteering experience and self-assessed skills as reviewed by a tutor. They are not a diploma or an official qualification.
